## Key Patterns & Insights

### 1. MIT dropout is the most common "signal"
- YC views MIT dropouts favorably
- MIT CS/Robotics dropouts are especially common
- "Dropped out of MIT to build" is practically a pattern

### 2. "Already founded a company / hit ARR in high school" is an emerging signal
- Edexia: $200K ARR at ages 17-18
- Parahelp: First startup at 13
- Datacurve: Collaborated with Team Canada in high school

### 3. PhD dropouts are also on the rise
- MIT Robotics PhD, Georgia Tech PhD, etc.
- Choosing execution/founding over research
- However, the overall PhD share is actually declining (6% → 4%)

### 4. Sharp rise in Stanford undergrads founding companies directly
- W24: 4.2% Stanford → W25: 9.7%
- "Stanford + ex-Google" is the most common profile

### 5. Med school/law school dropouts → AI/robotics
- RigManic: Oxford medical school → humanoid robots
- Unconventional, but entering the AI/hardware field

### 6. Serial founders with undergraduate origins
- Woz (Brad Eckert): MIT → YC S17 → reapplied for W25
- dawn (Alexis Gauba): Berkeley → founded Opyn (acquired by Coinbase) → W24
