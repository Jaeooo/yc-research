## Strengths of Korea/Asia Teams

### 1. Understanding of Asian markets
- Can specialize in Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia markets
- Understanding of local language/culture/business practices

### 2. Cross-border opportunities
- Business automation between Korea-US-Asia
- Supporting Asian companies seeking global expansion

### 3. Korean community
- Senior Korean founders already exist, e.g. Cuckoo Labs (W25)
- Can leverage the Korean network within YC

### 4. Technical capability + market access
- Korea's technical capability (semiconductors, IT infrastructure)
- Rapidly digitalizing market

---

## Recommended Directions

### 1. Cross-border AI Platform
- Business automation between Korea-US-Asia
- Multilingual AI agents
- Targeting Korean/Asian companies seeking global expansion

### 2. Asian Enterprise AI
- B2B AI specialized for Asian enterprises
- Korean/Japanese/Southeast Asian enterprise customers
- Localized AI solutions

### 3. K-Startup Global
- Supporting Korean startups' global expansion
- Automating overseas marketing and fundraising
- Synergy between US network and Korean network

### 4. Asia-focused Fintech
- Financial AI specialized for Asia
- Automating cross-border payments and currency exchange
- Leveraging accounting domain background

## Considerations

1. **Market size** — targeting only the Asian market could be limiting
2. **Competition** — need to compete with global players
3. **YC preference** — YC tends to favor companies with global market ambition
4. **Solution** — approach it as "starting in Asia, going global" rather than "Asia-specialized"
