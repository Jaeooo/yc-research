## Final Recommendation: Top 3 Positioning Strategies

### Strategy A: "AI Cross-border Trade Agent" (Category #1)
**Core idea:** An AI agent that fully automates B2B trade across Korea, the US, and Southeast Asia

- **Target:** Korean/Asian mid-sized companies seeking global expansion
- **Features:** Integrated quote → order → customs → payment → shipment tracking
- **Moat:** Korean trade data, customs databases, multilingual AI
- **Team role split:** one co-founder handles US market entry/global sales, the other handles Korean enterprise customers/technical development
- **Initial market:** ~50,000 Korean import/export companies

### Strategy B: "AI CFO for Asian SMEs" (Category #2)
**Core idea:** An AI financial/accounting platform specialized for Asian SMEs

- **Target:** Asian SMEs (3.5 million in Korea + Southeast Asia)
- **Features:** Multi-national accounting automation, tax calculation, cash flow forecasting
- **Moat:** Accounting expertise + knowledge of Asian accounting standards
- **Initial market:** Korean SMEs engaged in overseas trade

### Strategy C: "Asia Supply Chain AI" (Category #3)
**Core idea:** Integrated AI intelligence for Asian manufacturing supply chains

- **Target:** Manufacturing companies in Korea/China/Southeast Asia
- **Features:** Supply chain risk prediction, quality control, cost optimization
- **Moat:** Korean semiconductor/battery industry data
- **Initial market:** Supply chain management for Korean manufacturers

## Points to Prepare for the YC Interview

### Patterns YC likes
1. **Large market** — $10B+ TAM (all 3 strategies above satisfy this)
2. **Unique insight** — a clear answer to "why now? why you?"
3. **Early traction** — a plan for acquiring the first 10 customers
4. **Global expansion** — starting in Asia → going global

### Highlighting the strengths of a Korea/Asia team
- **Cuckoo Labs (W25) precedent:** proof that a Korean team can get into YC
- **Dual market access:** ability to approach Korea and the US simultaneously
- **Tech + business:** a combination of a technical lead and a business/accounting lead
- **Market timing:** the Asian AI market's 19.8% CAGR — now is the golden window
