## Key Insights

### 1. AI Agent/Automation is the largest category
- Over 30% of all AI+B2B companies
- Enterprise workflow automation and AI agents are the hottest area

### 2. AI Infra/Tooling is also strong
- Developer tools, APIs, platforms
- An area with high technical moats

### 3. Growth in Vertical AI
- AI specialized for specific industries like Fintech, Legal, Healthcare
- Domain expertise is a differentiator

### 4. Advantageous points for a Korean team
- Can serve as a bridgehead into the Asian market
- Targeting Korean/Asian enterprise customers
- Potential for multilingual/cross-border specialization

---

## Recommended Directions

1. **AI Agent for Asian Enterprises** — automation specialized for Asian enterprises
2. **Cross-border AI Tools** — business automation between Korea, the US, and Asia
3. **Vertical AI (Fintech/Legal)** — leveraging accounting domain knowledge
4. **AI Developer Tools** — leveraging technical capability
