---

## Recommended Strategy

### 1. AI + B2B direction
- YC's most-favored industry (62% of W25)
- B2B share is also at an all-time high of 61.4%

### 2. Target the Korean/Asian market
- A Korean founder is a differentiator
- Can serve as a bridgehead into the broader Asian market

### 3. Military service → leadership story
- A "non-traditional path" that YC favors
- Reference the 41 founders with military service

### 4. Leverage a rare school background
- BC alumni are a tiny minority among YC admits — usable as a "rarity" differentiator
- An accounting + CS combination is a pattern YC favors

### 5. Consider a leave of absence/dropping out
- YC favors teams that go "all in"
- 5.3% of founders are dropouts, and the share is rising

---

## Areas to Shore Up

| Weakness | Alternative strategy |
|------|-----------|
| **No Big Tech experience** | Substitute founding experience + technical skill |
| **No Stanford/MIT network** | Leverage the network of Korean YC founders |
| **No traction** | Move fast on an MVP and secure early users |

---

## Next Steps

1. **Finalize industry/idea direction** — AI + B2B recommended
2. **Build an MVP quickly** — admission is possible even at the idea stage
3. **Build a network of Korean YC founders** — reach out to Cuckoo Labs and others
4. **Prepare the YC application** — emphasize the military service story and the school-background combination
5. **Clarify roles within the team** — separate the business/finance lead from the technical lead
